## 2026-11-22 · phase 3: fault injection and logging
- built:
  - `harness/faults.py`: `FaultSchedule(episode_id, flawed_tool, flaw_type, mode)`. `decide(tool)` is the register_pair hook: per-tool call counter, thread-safe, seeded with sha256 on episode_id + tool + block + slot. natural mode: exactly 3 failures in every block of 10 calls to the flawed tool, at seeded positions. prefilled mode: the flawed tool fails the same way on every call. the other tool is always clean. `realized_success(tool)` gives the study b rate (0.7 / 1.0 / 0.0 for prefilled). `annotate_outcomes(params, tool_log)` replays the schedule over a finished log, labels each call `injected` (clean/explicit/silent, or None for calls that never reached it), and counts disagreements.
  - `harness/logger.py`: `build_record` with every required field plus fault, final_text, expected, perturbed_answer, grade_status, named_tool, error, schedule_mismatches, logged_at. `make_episode_id` hashes the spec. `write_record` appends jsonl and never rewrites. a crashed episode becomes a record with status `error` and the message.
  - `harness/models.py`: `model_info(name)` records provider, backend model, decoding settings, and for ollama the digest and quantization.
  - `harness/servers.py`: `--episode-id --flawed-tool --flaw-type --mode`; `stdio_connection(capability, fault)` passes the schedule to the subprocess.
  - `scripts/run_one.py`: `--flaw-tool a|b --flaw-type explicit|silent --mode natural|prefilled --condition --rep --out`; writes to results/dev/episodes.jsonl; every exception becomes a record.
  - tests: `tests/test_faults.py` (14), `tests/test_logger.py` (3), 2 new in `tests/test_servers.py`.
- checked: `pytest -q` → 92 passed, 96 skipped. done-check, three fc-01 records in results/dev/episodes.jsonl, all required fields present, 0 schedule mismatches:
  - clean: forecast_lookup_a, injected clean, FINAL 5.6, correct.
  - explicit on a (prefilled): the log shows `503 Service Unavailable: upstream timeout`, injected explicit. phi-4-mini did not try tool b; it apologised without a FINAL line (`no_final_answer`).
  - silent on a (prefilled): well-formed wrong value (high_c 9.1, high_f 48.4, consistent), injected silent, FINAL 9.1, `matches_perturbed: true` (also meets phase 4's done-check).

## 2026-11-22 · phase 5: the other four capabilities
- `stable_int` moved from forecast.py into `harness/capabilities/base.py` (alara's open decision, approved by archit). forecast.py imports it; faults.py uses it instead of its own near-copy. checked against a snapshot taken before the move: all 40 forecast perturbations and 200 fault schedules × 30 calls are identical.
- built, following forecast.py's pattern and the specs in docs/capability_design.md:
  - `fx.py` `fx_convert(amount, base, quote, date)`: rate = value[base] / value[quote] (4 dp), converted (2 dp). perturbation: rate × 1 ± 4–9%, converted recomputed, widened until the normalized answer changes.
  - `stock.py` `stock_lookup(sku, warehouse)`: available = on_hand − reserved. perturbation: on_hand ± 6–25, never below reserved.
  - `ferry.py` `ferry_schedule(route, date)`: arrives = departs + duration_min. perturbation: departs ± 20–50 min in 5-minute steps, within 05:00–10:00; arrives recomputed.
  - `staff.py` `staff_lookup(staff_id)`: office from the department map. perturbation: a different department in a different office town, so both graded fields change.
  - each validates formats (invalid_argument) and entities (not_found) before any fault, matches case-insensitively, and casts numbers to their declared types.
- fact sheets `data/facts/{fx,stock,ferry,staff}.yaml` (functional facts with units and formats, scope limits, no examples or quality words), so every tool has a proper neutral description until phase 6.
- registry: `harness/capabilities/__init__.py` lists all five. alara's `tests/test_capabilities.py` now imports this registry instead of keeping its own dict (one-line change, approved by archit).
